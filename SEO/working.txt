How Google Search Basically Works

You should understand these 3 words first:

1.Crawling

Google uses automated programs called crawlers to discover pages.

Google
   ↓
Crawler
   ↓
Your website
   ↓
Reads pages
2.Indexing

Google stores information about your page in its index.

Your page
   ↓
Google understands it
   ↓
Google Index
3.Ranking

When somebody searches, Google decides which pages should appear first.

User searches
      ↓
Google
      ↓
Search results
      ↓
Rank #1
Rank #2
Rank #3
...

So:

Crawl → Index → Rank

This is one of the most important concepts in SEO.

4.The 4 Major Parts of SEO

You'll hear these terms constantly.

🟢 On-Page SEO

Things you control inside your website.

Examples:

Page title
Meta description
Headings
Content
Keywords
Images
Internal links
URLs
Structured data
🔵 Technical SEO

Things related to the technical structure of your website.

Examples:

Website speed
Mobile friendliness
HTTPS
Sitemap
robots.txt
Canonical URLs
Crawlability
Core Web Vitals
JavaScript rendering
Proper redirects
Server configuration

This part will be particularly useful for you because you're working with Next.js/React/VPS/Nginx.

🟠 Off-Page SEO

Things happening outside your website.

The biggest example:

Backlinks

Suppose:

Website A
   ↓
links to
   ↓
Your website

Google can treat that as a signal that your website is worth considering.

But:

Not all backlinks are good.

10 high-quality relevant backlinks can be much more valuable than hundreds of spammy links.

🟣 Local SEO

Very important if you're building websites for local businesses.

Example:

"Best bakery in Madurai"

or

"Web developer in Coimbatore"

Local SEO involves things such as:

Google Business Profile
Business information
Location pages
Reviews
Local citations
NAP consistency

NAP means:

Name + Address + Phone
